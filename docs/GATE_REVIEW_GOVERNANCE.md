# Stage-gate review governance

## Purpose

A stage may be marked complete only through a review record that names the condition, evidence references, and remaining limitation. The review engine rejects complete status with no evidence or references to files that do not exist.

## Scope and honesty limit

This is evidence traceability, not automated scientific peer review. Existing files can demonstrate that artifacts were produced; they do not, by themselves, validate the proposed plasma physics or establish independent replication.

Independent science, mission safety, security, and data-governance review remain required at the relevant gates.

## Status rules

- complete: evidence references are required and verified to exist; limitations remain visible.
- in_progress: work is active; no completion claim.
- blocked: an explicit missing authority, evidence, source, or condition prevents advance.

The research-status snapshot reads these validated records. A manual UI change cannot bypass the gate-review data model.
