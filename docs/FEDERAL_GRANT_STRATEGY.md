## 2. NASA SBIR Subtopic Z1.03 Executive Abstract (Ready to Submit)

```text
PROPOSAL TITLE:
HEIMDALL: In-Situ Electrostatic Plasma Wake Profiler for Hypervelocity Sub-Centimeter Orbital Debris Characterization

TECHNICAL ABSTRACT:
NASA’s Orbital Debris Program Office (ODPO) identifies 0.1 mm to 1 cm kinetic impactors as the dominant unmitigated collision hazard for crewed and robotic LEO missions. Ground-based radars (Space Fence, Haystack LRIR) suffer from severe Rayleigh backscatter decay (D^6), creating a catastrophic blind spot for particles smaller than 3 cm. Project HEIMDALL addresses Topic Z1.03 by exploiting the supersonic Mach cone shockwave generated by hypervelocity debris transiting the LEO ionospheric plasma.

Because electrostatic wake disturbance scales with physical cross-sectional area (D^2), HEIMDALL delivers a 12 dB/octave physical sensitivity advantage over terrestrial radar, establishing a reliable detection threshold down to 0.1 mm. Under Phase I, the team will:
1. Benchmark 3D Particle-in-Cell (PIC) wake dynamics against IRI-2020 ionosphere profiles.
2. Characterize multi-channel analog front-end (AFE) noise floors in simulated plasma vacuum environments.
3. Validate FPGA wavelet pulse compression algorithms capable of 24:1 telemetry bandwidth reduction.

Phase I culminates in an end-to-end TRL 4 hardware-in-the-loop breadboard, paving the way for an on-orbit 3U CubeSat demonstration flight under Phase II via the CubeSat Launch Initiative (CSLI).
```
