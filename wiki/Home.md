## Welcome to the HEIMDALL ELECTRA Research Wiki

Welcome to the definitive scientific wiki and technical compendium for **Project HEIMDALL ELECTRA**, a pioneering space situational awareness (SSA) research initiative created by **Aarti S. Ravikumar**. 

This wiki documents the mathematical derivations, plasma physics formulations, multi-node kinematic TDOA solvers, conjunction assessment risk models, and fail-closed evidence ledgers designed to track untracked, sub-decimeter ($1\text{–}10\text{ cm}$) space debris in Low Earth Orbit (LEO).
